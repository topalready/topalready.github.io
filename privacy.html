Privacy and terms

The short version: we don't know who you are, we don't track you for ads, and any photo can be hidden with one tap.

What the numbers mean

The percentage for each project is a time-based estimate: how much of the time between launch and the expected completion date has passed. It is not a measurement of work on site, and it is not official information from HDB or any developer.

Dates come from public sources and may be out of date or wrong. Before making any decision, check with HDB or the developer. Nothing here is financial, legal or property advice.

Site photos

  • Photos must be taken within 800 m of the site and within the last 14 days. Your phone checks this on the spot.
  • Your exact location is never sent or stored. We keep only the photo, the date it was taken and roughly how far from the site it was.
  • Photos are resized before upload, which removes the hidden camera and location details inside the file.
  • Photograph the building. No faces, car number plates, people's homes or anything offensive.
  • Anyone can tap Report on a photo, and it is hidden straight away while it's checked. We may remove any photo at any time.
  • You keep the copyright to your photo. By uploading it, you let TOP Already? show it in the app and in progress cards people share.

Usage counts

To see whether the app is useful, we count simple events: a visit, a project opened, a photo added, a card shared. These counts have no name, account, phone number or device ID attached. There are no cookies and no advertising trackers.

The companies that host the app (GitHub for the website, Supabase for photos and counts) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, as most websites do.

Saved on your phone

Your watchlist, the sites you've "found" on the map, the photos you've reported and your day or night setting are saved only in this browser on this device. There's no account, so nothing is backed up.

They can be lost if you clear this browser's data, use private browsing, or switch phone or browser. On iPhone, Safari may clear a website's saved data after seven days of Safari use without opening that website. Apps added to the Home Screen are exempt from that rule, but this still isn't a backup.

Where the information comes from

BTO dates and announced delays: BTO HQ. Block and postal details: OneMap. Condo launch and expected TOP dates: EdgeProp listings. Map: OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap contributors. Site photos: people near each site.

Removing something

To remove a photo, tap Report on it and it disappears immediately. If it's your photo and you want it deleted for good, report it and it will be removed when checked.
